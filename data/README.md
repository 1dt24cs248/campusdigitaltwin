# Synthetic Demo Data

These CSVs are generated by `scripts/generate_demo_data.py` with a fixed random
seed. They describe a fictional campus and are not real campus records, meter
readings, attendance observations, or computer-vision results.

From the workspace root, generate or regenerate the files with:

```powershell
python campusdigitaltwin/scripts/generate_demo_data.py
```

The generator creates:

- 3 fictional buildings and 12 rooms (11 timetable-eligible).
- 30 normal class entries across weekdays and non-overlapping time slots.
- 24 equipment records, with quantity-adjusted total wattage.
- 12 energy estimates based on equipment power and assumed daily hours.
- 12 occupancy snapshots labeled `synthetic_demo_data`; confidence is blank
  because these rows were not produced by a detector.
- 4 separate reassignment scenarios, including conflict, capacity, and
  ineligible-room cases. The normal timetable remains valid.
- 7 sustainability-action descriptions supporting missing/stale data, equipment
  runtime review, tariff verification, allocation checks, and simulator review.

The default electricity tariff is INR 8.50/kWh and is an illustrative,
unverified assumption. Override it using `--tariff`; change reproducibility with
`--seed`; use `--output-dir` to write elsewhere. The script validates all
room/building references, timetable room eligibility and capacity, timetable
conflicts, equipment arithmetic, energy cost arithmetic, and occupancy bounds
before writing the CSVs.

`estimated_connected_load_kw` is the sum of inventory `total_power_watts` for
each room. Daily energy is the sum of equipment kW multiplied by each record's
`assumed_daily_hours`; weekly energy is five assumed operating days. The values
are estimates, not meter data.
